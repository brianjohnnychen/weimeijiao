<h2 id="intro">Four keys to family rules</h2>

Family rules work best when there are few of them, they are said positively, you make them together and you follow through.

- **Keep it short.** Choose the 3 to 5 rules that matter most to your family. Cross out the ones on the poster that don't fit and write your own on the blank lines.
- **Say what to do.** "Gentle hands" tells a child what to do. "Don't hit" only says what to stop.
- **Make them together.** Talk about the rules at a calm time and let your child suggest the wording. Everyone signs, grown-ups too.
- **Follow through.** Notice and name it when your child follows a rule. When a rule is broken, give one calm reminder, then follow through with the consequence you agreed on, every time.
