<h2 id="intro">How to print</h2>

There are four kinds of sheet here: the age finder quiz, a one-page summary for each age, a learning-at-home one-pager for each age, and three everyday tools (a routine chart, a calm-down plan and a family rules poster). Each prints straight from its page, or saves as a PDF.

- **Print from the page.** Open the printable and press Print, or use your browser's print command (Ctrl+P on Windows, Command+P on a Mac). Only the sheet prints; the site's menus and buttons are left out.
- **Download the PDF.** Press Download PDF to keep a copy on your phone or computer. The PDFs are A4 size. On US Letter paper, choose "Fit to page" or a similar scaling option in the print dialog.
- **No color printer needed.** Black and white is fine.
- **Print a few copies.** Use a fresh routine chart each week, or slip one into a clear plastic sleeve and check the boxes with a dry-erase marker so you can wipe it and use it again.
- **Hang it where your child can see it.** Put the routine chart, calm-down plan and family rules poster at your child's eye level.
- **Take the quiz on screen.** The age finder works out the result for you online, and the printed version comes with a scoring key.
