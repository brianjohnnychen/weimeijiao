<h2 id="intro">How to use this quiz</h2>

Answer 10 questions about what your child does now, from walking and talking to school, reading and friends. If you are not sure, pick the closest answer.

Children develop at different speeds, and one child can be ahead in one area and behind in another. This quiz only points you to a page to start with. It is not a developmental assessment, and it cannot tell you whether your child is on track. You can also read the stages just before and after your result, or pick the stage that sounds most like your child on the <L to="/by-age/#phases">By age</L> page. If you are worried about your child's development, talk to your pediatrician.
