Wei Mei Jiao is built by Brian, a dad raising two daughters with his wife. Their family moved from Boston to Taiwan to be closer to Brian's parents. The site gathers research on disciplining young children and turns it into steps you can use in a hard moment. <L to="/about/#story">Read Brian's story</L>.

The site cites academic sources only: peer-reviewed studies, systematic reviews and meta-analyses, and policy statements that medical and scientific bodies published in journals. Whenever a page says what works, what harms or what is normal at an age, a small number links to the full entry on the Research page. Where findings are mixed or researchers disagree, the page says so. <L to="/research/#how-to-read">How to read the research</L>.

This is educational information, not medical or psychological advice. If you are worried about your child, see <L to="/about/#get-help">when to get professional help</L>.
