<h2 id="intro">How to use the calm-down plan</h2>

Fill it in together when everyone is calm, not in the middle of a meltdown. Ask your child what their body feels like when they are upset and what helps them feel better. Tick the ideas that fit and write in their own words. Children who can't write yet can draw a picture in each box.

Practice the calming ideas a few times while things are calm, so they feel familiar when feelings run high.

Keep the plan where your child can see it. When a big feeling starts, stay close, point to the plan and let your child choose. This fits with <L to="/toolbox/#time-in">time-in</L>, where you stay with your child while they calm down. For what to do in the middle of a tantrum, see <L to="/situations/#tantrums-now">Tantrums: right now</L>.
