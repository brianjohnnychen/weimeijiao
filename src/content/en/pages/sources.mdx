<h2 id="intro">About this list</h2>

Every source is a peer-reviewed journal article, a systematic review or meta-analysis, a Cochrane review, a university-press book or a policy statement that a scientific or medical body published in a peer-reviewed journal. Each one was opened and checked before it was cited, and an automated check confirms every week that each DOI still resolves to the right work. The <L to="/research/#how-to-read">Research page</L> explains the study types and how to weigh them.
